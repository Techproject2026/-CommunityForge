Why you should list in CommunityForge?
listing your items and skills helps reduce waste, saves your neighbors money, and fosters strong local friendships. By sharing what you have, you help build a more sustainable and supportive community!
Why you should you use CommunityForge?
Using CommunityForge will help you save money and make new friends and we would love for everyone in our community to be together and help when needed and will make your social interaction better.
What if my leaner/borrower doesn't contact me?
In a case where your borrower doesn't contact you, that would be an issue that you communicate directly through the contact details provided in their email request. Check your spam folders or reach out using their provided phone number.
What if my borrower doesn't give me the item before the due date?
Communicate openly and check in with your borrower. Often, unexpected scheduling delays happen, and a quick text message or phone call is all it takes to resolve the return timeframe smoothly.
What if you have found a item or a skill from someone else that you know and you would like to unskill them or unborrow that?
Communicate with the original lister to let them know plans have changed. They can update their availability on their profile, or you can send a quick note to cancel the arrangement so other neighbors can utilize the item or skill instead. We don't have unborrow button because if we did it would of not increase your socializing and reduse it instead.
